# Privacy Policy Template for PredictVola

**Last Updated:** August 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how the **PredictVola** application ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. We are committed to safeguarding user privacy.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
PredictVola is designed as an informational tool. We respect your privacy and therefore:
* We **do not** require you to create an account or sign in.
* We **do not** collect personal identification information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address).
* We **do not** access or collect device contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location data.
* We **do not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data with any third parties.

## 3. Information Collected Automatically
When you use the App, the App communicates with Google Firebase Firestore to fetch the latest publicly available stock volatility metrics. Google Cloud services may collect anonymous technical telemetry (such as IP address, operating system version, and standard network connection data) as necessary for transmitting network packets and maintaining service availability. For more details on Google's privacy practices, visit [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. External Links
The App contains links to external websites for methodology, disclosures, and related documentation. If you click on an external link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We encourage you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
