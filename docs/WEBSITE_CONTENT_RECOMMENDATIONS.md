## 2. Recommended Key Content Sections

### A. Explaining the Color Indicator to Users
* **Yellowish Tile (`IV < Predicted Volatility`)**:
  * *Market Interpretation:* The current options market is pricing in a lower volatility than what the quantitative model forecasts over the upcoming 5-day horizon. This can indicate underpriced options premium or potential upcoming volatility expansion.
* **Greenish Tile (`IV >= Predicted Volatility`)**:
  * *Market Interpretation:* Market implied volatility meets or exceeds the predicted volatility, suggesting standard pricing or expected volatility contraction.

### B. Methodology & Data Sources
* **Data Sources:** End-of-day market and implied volatility data powered by [massive.com](https://massive.com/).
* **Computation Time:** Automatically processed daily post-market close (16:30 EST) via Google Cloud Infrastructure.
* **Algorithm:** GARCH(1,1) autoregressive conditional heteroskedasticity / econometric forecast modeling.

### C. Regulatory & Financial Risk Disclaimer
> **Important Disclaimer:** *All volatility forecasts, implied volatility values, and stock price information presented in this application are generated by automated statistical algorithms for informational and educational purposes only. They do not constitute investment, financial, tax, or legal advice, nor an endorsement or solicitation to buy or sell any security, option contract, or financial instrument. Trading stocks and options involves substantial risk of loss.*

### D. Privacy Policy Highlights
Google Play Store requires a publicly accessible privacy policy link. Key points:
* The app does not require account registration or login.
* No personally identifiable information (PII), location data, or contact data is collected or stored.
* All network traffic is secure (HTTPS / Firestore TLS encryption).
